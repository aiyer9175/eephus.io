# Trust Matrix (Phase 1 pilot)

Trust vocabulary matches `milb-projections/definitions/COVERAGE_AND_TRUST.md`: `TRUSTED | USABLE_WITH_FILTER | PARTIAL | UNAVAILABLE` (no venue in this pilot showed `KNOWN_DEFECT`-style partial-field corruption -- coverage was clean rate-based, not malformed). Assigned per (level, venue, field) from the measured coverage rate; see TRUST_MATRIX.json for the full per-field breakdown.
