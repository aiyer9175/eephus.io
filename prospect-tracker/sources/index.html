PROSPX grades are a consensus of seven industry sources. A player's card shows each source's own FV grade beside the consensus, and excerpts of the scouting reports FanGraphs, MLB Pipeline, Baseball America, ESPN and Just Baseball have published on that player: the opening of each report, at most 280 characters, labelled with its source, list and publication date, with a View on link to the player's page at the source where we know it and otherwise to the source's page for that list. The draft board shows excerpts of the draft reports from FanGraphs, MLB Pipeline and Baseball America the same way. The full reports stay with their publishers. Trademarks, data and report text belong to their owners.

Sources with a question or concern: arkndjl@gmail.com.

Building on the data: the data we publish.

What each source supplies, in full

FanGraphs

Prospect rankings, FV grades, tool grades, and prospect and draft scouting reports (one of the seven PROSPX consensus inputs); league stats and park factors.

MLB Pipeline

Prospect rankings, grades, and prospect and draft scouting reports (consensus input); draft bonus pools and slot values.

Baseball America

Prospect rankings, grades, and prospect and draft scouting reports (consensus input).

ESPN

Prospect rankings and scouting reports (consensus input); the club logo images on team pickers and boards load from ESPN's image server (the logos are the clubs' trademarks).

Just Baseball

Prospect rankings and scouting reports (consensus input).

TJStats

Prospect rankings (consensus input) and movers wire.

MLB Stats API / Baseball Savant

Game logs, transactions, rosters, the sabermetrics feed (league wOBA constants; the fielding and framing runs behind DEM+), Statcast tracking data (pitch, batted-ball and zone metrics), and player headshots via MLB static media; on the Scores / Standings page, live scores, win probability, box scores and highlight video played in MLB's own embedded player. The Awards / HoF page reads the writers' MVP, Cy Young, Rookie and Manager of the Year results from MLB's awards endpoint.

Baseball-Reference

war_daily (bWAR): the run frame the served eWAR is built on and the source the Hall of Fame board reproduces, plus historical player pages.

pybaseball

Open-source Python toolkit used to pull Statcast, FanGraphs, and Baseball-Reference data programmatically.

Spotrac

Contracts, payrolls, free-agent classes, and international signing trackers.

MLB Trade Rumors

Arbitration salary projections (Matt Swartz's model) and the arbitration results tracker: the comparator /arbitration/ scores its own model against, and a record of settled salaries the arbitration dataset reads.

The Odds API

Sportsbook moneylines, run lines and totals for the /betting/ board, and the game lines /fantasy/ DFS pricing reads.

National Weather Service

Game-time weather forecasts (the hourly forecast at each US park, from api.weather.gov), captured daily for a pre-registered betting candidate; no page shows them yet. NWS data is a US Government work in the public domain and needs no attribution line; we credit it here and claim none of it as our own.

The data we publish

contents, cadence and stability of every served artifact

Every model writes a static JSON file under /data/; the site reads the same files. No key, no quota, no sign-up. 65 artifacts documented below, 26 of them stable.

Not promised: version headers, deprecation windows, uptime, or byte layout. Fields can be added without notice. A field named here keeps its name and meaning.

39 more files stay local (model internals and pipeline inputs no page fetches) and are not listed.

CSV from any board

Download CSV writes what is on screen: current sort, filters and display formats. Use the artifacts for whole boards.

eFV movement alerts: feed only, no delivery

Each rebuild publishes the biggest eFV moves, with drivers, to /data/efv_alerts.json. Nothing is emailed or pushed; the artifact's delivery field says so.

A move with an endpoint at the eFV 20 floor is a player entering or leaving the graded pool, not a re-grade; filter on at_floor.

Pitcher and hitter grades at equal FV

on change · 6 KB · as of August 12, 2026

eFV vs a rebuilt consensus

The fairness control behind the head-to-head claim. eFV is normally compared against the consensus AS SERVED, so part of its lead could be eFV beating the blending rather than beating the scouts. This rebuilds the consensus from measured per-source precision and re-runs the comparison on identical rows, under both registered noise-floor estimators, with the decision rule fixed in advance and two adversarial reweighting controls.

/data/xfv_denoised_baseline_eval.json
on change · 13 KB · as of August 11, 2026

experimental: shape can change or the file can disappear on any run; current shape pinned by a test.
`basis.vintage_disciplined` is FALSE by construction and the number must never be quoted as out of sample: per-source precision is estimable only for board years 2023-2026, so the baseline is defined using rows it also scores. Read `primary_corrected_amendment_2`, not `primary_as_registered`.

Which frame the head-to-head is scored on

eFV carries one grade per player, fitted at that player's decision year, while the common set carries a consensus read for every base year. That makes the comparison frame a choice, and the choice decides the answer. This scores three frames inside the same bootstrap draws: every base year, post-debut reads removed, and base year equal to the decision year. Each gets its own floor.

/data/xfv_headtohead_frame_eval.json
on change · 4 KB · as of August 13, 2026

experimental: shape can change or the file can disappear on any run; current shape pinned by a test.
The middle frame is the only one that clears a floor and it is deliberately not the headline: it removes the rows where the consensus is advantaged while keeping those where eFV is, so its margin is partly an artifact of that asymmetry. Read `vintage_matched`, which is the symmetric contest and reads a tie at the smallest floor of the three.
