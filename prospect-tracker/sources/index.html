Data Sources & Attribution

every upstream the platform reads, and how it is used

eephus.io is a sabermetric analysis platform. PROSPX grades are a consensus built across seven independent industry sources - the value added is the aggregation, calibration, and track-record audit, not any single list. No source's rankings or reports are republished wholesale; excerpts appear with attribution in the spirit of commentary and analysis. All trademarks and data remain the property of their owners.

If you represent one of these sources and have a question or concern, email arkndjl@gmail.com and it will be addressed promptly.

FanGraphs

Prospect rankings, FV grades, tool grades, and scouting reports (one of the seven PROSPX consensus inputs); league stats and park factors.

MLB Pipeline

Prospect rankings and grades (consensus input); draft bonus pools and slot values.

Baseball America

Prospect rankings and grades (consensus input).

ESPN

Prospect rankings (consensus input); team logo imagery on internal surfaces.

The Athletic

Prospect rankings (consensus input).

Just Baseball

Prospect rankings (consensus input).

TJStats

Prospect rankings (consensus input) and movers wire.

MLB Stats API / Baseball Savant

Game logs, transactions, rosters, Statcast tracking data (pitch, batted-ball, and zone metrics), and player headshots via MLB static media.

Baseball-Reference

bWAR - the WAR framework used site-wide - plus historical player pages.

pybaseball

Open-source Python toolkit used to pull Statcast, FanGraphs, and Baseball-Reference data programmatically.

Spotrac

Contracts, payrolls, free-agent classes, and international signing trackers.
