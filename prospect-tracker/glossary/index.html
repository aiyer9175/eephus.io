Glossary & Methodology

every term, model derivation, and the track record behind the site

Every number on eephus.io resolves to an entry on this page. The glossary is the platform's single source of truth: the PROSPX board, the Free Agency module, the command palette (Ctrl+K from anywhere), and every hover definition read from the same file that renders these cards.

The analytical frame throughout is resource allocation. A prospect is a cost-controlled asset whose scouting grade implies a production forecast; a free agent is a priced asset whose contract is the market's forecast. For any priced decision the platform keeps two independently derived numbers - what the production is worth (rational value, surplus) and what the market pays (predicted or actual contract) - and never blends them, because the gap between them is the entire point.

WAR site-wide is eWAR (eephus WAR): Baseball-Reference run frame with pitching re-priced on FIP; fielding unchanged pending SDR; position players match bWAR except in seasons they also pitched. One series, every surface. Where samples are thin the models shrink toward the mean and say so; where data is missing the site shows a plain dash, never a guess.

Model derivations and the interactive teaching widgets live under Methodology; the source-vs-source backtest under Source Comparison grades the site's own consensus against its inputs - misses included. Source Accuracy goes one level finer and asks which outlet grades which kind of prospect better; on 21 of 25 scored cohort cells the honest answer is that the data cannot tell, and the view says so in every row rather than crowning the larger of two statistically identical numbers. No blend weight ships from it.
