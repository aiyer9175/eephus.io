Questions

Is any of this gated right now?

No. Every Diamond page is public and free while we build it: no paywall, no card form, no trial. Any change is announced before it happens.

Will the price go up?

List price is $15 a month. Founding members pay $9.99 for twelve months and are not moved if the list price rises above $15 in that year.

Where is the Card Market?

Removed on 2026-09-05: it ran on public sold prices with no licensed feed, and no feasible source turned up. It is not a member benefit and returns only if a licensed price source is wired.

Do you guarantee I will make money betting?

No. The record is built to be audited: each published pick carries its timestamp, book and odds, a flat one-unit stake, nothing deleted, each graded on realized results.

What happens to the free site?

It stays free.

Who is behind this?

One person writes the models; a small group writes the articles. Every model publishes its eval artifacts, failed experiments included.
