Diamond Pass ◆ Diamond preview

The paid tier that keeps the rest of eephus.io free. It is not selling anything yet.

Founding preview: everything is free while we build

Diamond is not switched on. Every page in this section is open to everyone right now. Use them, tell us what is wrong with them, and you will be a founding member when the doors open.

eephus.io is a sabermetrics site that publishes its own report card. The prospect model posts its out-of-sample accuracy against the public consensus, and it posts the experiments that failed too. That habit is the product.

Diamond is the paid tier on top of it: the fantasy and betting work. Everything else on the site stays free forever. Diamond funds the free site.

$15 a month, when it goes live

Nothing is for sale today. When Diamond opens, it is $15 a month, and everything else on eephus.io stays free. Not free for now. Free.

The first group of members gets a founding rate of $9.99 a month, held for a year. If you are reading this during the preview, you are in that group.

Diamond funds the free site. The board, the projections, the free agency model, the glossary and every article stay open to everyone, and the people paying for Diamond are what keeps them that way.

What members get

two modules, plus four things that are not a module, each labeled with what actually exists today

Fantasy

Three surfaces on one board set. Redraft: rest-of-season ranks in your scoring format, daily streamers, two-start pitchers, buy and sell. Dynasty: the full keeper board with the eFV blend and the keeper calculator. DFS: per-game projections for the slate and the lineup optimizer, run against salaries you import.

Sports Betting

Model prices on every game, the pick of the day, the calculators, and the public record graded against the close.

Data exports and a JSON API

live now

Every model artifact the site runs on is public static JSON, now with a documented catalog: what is in each file, how often it updates, and which parts are stable enough to build on. Every board also carries a Download CSV control that writes exactly the rows you are looking at.

eFV movement alerts

feed only

The feed is built and published on every rebuild: the biggest genuine eFV moves with the driver behind each one. Delivery is not wired. Telling us which players you follow needs the account layer, which is not switched on yet, so today you read the feed rather than being told.

The DFS lineup optimizer

live now

A salary-cap solver over our per-game projections. You bring the salaries from your own site.

A members-only weekly newsletter

planned, not built

Not built. There is no list, no signup and no first issue, and it will not be described as a benefit until there is.

Questions worth asking

including the ones a sales page would skip

Is any of this gated right now?

No. Every page in the Diamond section is public and free while we build it. There is no paywall in the code path, no card form, and no trial that quietly converts. When that changes, it will be announced before it happens, not after.

Will the price go up?

The list price is $15 a month. Founding members pay $9.99 and keep that for twelve months. If the price ever moves above $15, founding members are not moved with it during that year.

Where is the Card Market?

Removed on 2026-09-05. The screen ran on public sold prices with no licensed feed behind it, no feasible source turned up, and the module came off the site and the nightly pipeline. It is not a member benefit and will not be described as one unless a licensed price source is wired.

Do you guarantee I will make money betting?

No, and anyone who does is lying to you. What we promise instead is a record you can audit: every pick posted with its timestamp, book and odds, a flat one-unit stake, nothing deleted after the fact, and every pick graded against the closing line so you can see whether the model actually knew something or just got lucky.

What happens to the free site?

It stays free. The prospect board, the projections, the free agency model, the glossary and the articles are the reason anyone shows up, and putting them behind a wall would be the fastest way to kill the thing. Diamond funds the free site.

Who is behind this?

One person writing the models and a small group writing the articles. The models publish their own eval artifacts, including the experiments that failed, which is not something a marketing page usually offers to show you.

There is nothing to buy yet

There is no signup, because accounts are not switched on and the preview needs none. When accounts open, founding status will be honored for the people who were here during the preview.
