Losses

  • our SERVED estimator against the PUBLISHED pre-settlement Swartz projection, on settled figures, identical rows: This model does not beat the MLB Trade Rumors / Matt Swartz projection. Measured on identical rows, Swartz is materially more accurate at every arbitration step. Reported as a NULL. Swartz uses counting stats (HR, RBI, saves, wins) this model excludes; how much of the gap that explains is not measured. Swartz is the better estimate of a KNOWN season; it is not available for an unplayed one.
  • the model beats the step-multiplier baseline on arb 2/3/4: The arb-2-and-later model is a NULL. Its margin over a median step multiplier off the prior salary is below the floor measured for this basis. The A3 control shows the production block does carry signal there - permuting it makes the model WORSE than the baseline - so this is a power limit, not an absence of information, and it is still reported as a null.

What this model cannot tell you

  • It prices the SYSTEM, not the hearing: no filed figures, so a panel case is scored on its award alone.
  • Steps before 2022 are reconstructed from substitute service-time sources: reported, NOT scored.
  • 2020 is never a label year (salaries paid at 60/162, reported on inconsistent bases).
  • The CBA expires 2026-12-01. Seasons from 2027 assume the arbitration structure survives; no interval here covers that.
  • Non-tendered players are absent, so summed projections OVERSTATE what a club owes.
