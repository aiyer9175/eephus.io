Fantasy ◆ Diamond preview

Redraft, dynasty and DFS on one projection engine.Part of Diamond. Free during the founding preview.
Rest-of-season ranks and daily streamers in your scoring format (Ottoneu FanGraphs Points default, SABR Points, Old School 5x5, Ottoneu 4x4). Projections from the MLB Projections engine; matchup context from REM+, SEM+, HEM+, Offense+ and Bullpen+.
Dynasty and keeper value: prospects on eFV, blended with ETA and surplus so prospects and MLB players share one ranking. Call-up Watch flags who is next; the keeper calculator prices years of control against redraft cost.
Today's slate: the Redraft engine re-scored per game for the probable pitcher, park and bullpen. Paste or import your DFS salaries and the optimizer builds against them. No salary feed is scraped.
