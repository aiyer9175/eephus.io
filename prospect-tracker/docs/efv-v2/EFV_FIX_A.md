## 2. The flags and who flips them

- `efv_w2_popmle.POP_REPAIR_ON = False`. With it False every served path, the production fit
  and every artifact are byte-identical to the code before this task. EFV_FIX_B_REFIT turns it
  on in the same commit as its registered re-fit; it never flips alone, because the production
  heads refit on every export and the popmle column moves their slopes.
- `efv_dataset.LEVEL_BLEND_ANCHOR_MOST_EXPOSED = False`, unchanged. The cameo rule is the arm's
  new definition; the arm reaches the board only on the B3 gate's own read.

## 3. What is measured here (descriptive, outcome-blind)

No outcome, label residual, Spearman against WAR or grade is computed. No list vintage 2020 or
2021 and no 2025 or later debut cohort is read. Nothing is grouped, counted or averaged by
consensus-FV level (`docs/PROSPECT_ANCHOR_GATE.md`).

1. Norms (A1). Per population season 2005 to 2019 and per live season 2021 to 2026, the AAA
   hitter K/PA reference rate under the legacy and the repaired rule (their ratio), and the
   exposure-weighted mean of every `*_rel` feature over LINE_MIN rows of each side under both.
2. Mexican League (A6, POP-2 verification before the change is used). Per season, the count of
   `league == "MEX"` lines in the sport-11 cache files, their share of AAA lines, and the
   population rows (LINE_MIN, pre-debut) whose season includes one.
3. Column changes (A1 + A6). The `pop_proj_war` column on the production training rows (the v1
   matrix rows with decision year before the R-VM cohort, read as production_columns reads
   them) and on the live board (read year list year + 1, cutoff today, as `_fam_popmle` reads
   it), legacy against repaired: rows with a value, mean and sd, Spearman of old against new,
   median and max absolute change, the largest live movers in value units. Each translator is
   refit by its own construction; the heads are not refit.
4. Cameo (A5). On the live matrices built in memory, the count of rows per side whose
   `level_rank` would move under the cameo arm, and the step sizes. Built with `persist=False`;
   no matrix on disk changes.

Every number lands in the task's report and commit messages; none lands in a served artifact,
the progress ledger or the Source Comparison chart.

## 5. Results (2026-10-15, after the registration commit 17eb92d6)

Outcome-blind, as registered. Commits: bf6c1bef (A1, A6), c552f61e (A5), 1fed42c8 (A3).

- Norms (A1). AAA hitter K/PA legacy against repaired: 2005 0.041 / 0.179, 2015 0.044 / 0.187,
  2019 0.052 / 0.224, 2026 0.056 / 0.223 (ratio exactly 0.25 from 2021; about 0.23 before, where
  the repaired value also drops the Mexican League). The LINE_MIN exposure-weighted 2026 means of
  hitter k_rel, bb_rel, hr_rel, sb_rel move from 0.174, 0.094, 0.019, 0.032 to 0.000; pitcher
  k_rel and bb_rel from 0.117 and 0.061 to 0.001 and -0.001.
- Mexican League (A6, POP-2 verified). League MEX sits in every sport-11 population season but
  2018: 288 to 350 hitting lines (19 to 23 percent of AAA lines), 312 to 411 pitching lines (26 to
  29 percent), about 200 hitter and 230 pitcher population rows a season. The live fit's rows go
  47,487 to 44,536 (hit) and 48,895 to 45,669 (pit).
- Column changes (A1 and A6 together, heads not refit). Production training column: Spearman old
  against new 0.904 (hit, n 1,230) and 0.913 (pit, n 1,098). Live column: 0.796 (hit, n 2,127,
  largest change 3.52, luke_adams 3.86 to 7.38) and 0.874 (pit, n 2,366). nelson_rada 7.69 to 7.48.
- Cameo (A5). With the arm on, 160 of 2,142 live hitters (150 down one level, 9 down two, 1 down
  three) and 220 of 2,409 pitchers (216 down one, 4 down two) would move level_rank.
- Served proof (A3). data/xfv.json by_player identical to the prior commit (9,515 rows); the only
  meta change is served_model.disclosures.
