# The 2025 and 2026 pitch-event pools for the live eFV read (rule, fixed before any number)

Written 2026-10-15 (task EFV_FIX_A_PBP_2026) under the owner's decision EW3-21 of
2026-10-15 (`_audit_2026-09-09/DECISIONS_FOR_OWNER.md`; the eFV driver audit, defect D6:
the pitch-event family is dark on the served board) and the waiver of registered dates
EW3-5 (2026-10-14). This document is committed ALONE, before the 2026 pitch events are
fetched and before any census, coverage or aggregate number of 2025 or 2026 is read. No
em-dashes. Nothing here is cut by consensus-FV cell (`docs/PROSPECT_ANCHOR_GATE.md`): the
only groups are side, level and season.

## 1. The defect

The live board reads list year 2026 at read year 2027 as of today, so
`efv_w2_pbp.read_seasons` asks for seasons 2026 and 2025. `efv_w2_pbp.AGG_SEASONS` ends at
2024 (the last season any development or reserved read could see when the family was
built), so every live profile is None and `efv5_combined._fam_pbp` serves the family dark
on both sides. The served pitcher order is therefore a reduced model: the fitted pitcher
skill slopes on csw and k_resid (3.77 and 3.27 percentile points per SD in the audit)
multiply an imputed z of 0 on every live row.

## 2. What is built, and how

1. The fetch (`milb_pbp_fetch.py`): the 2026 final regular-season schedule of sport ids
   11 (AAA), 12 (AA), 13 (A+), 14 (A) and 16 (ROK), then every game's compact pitch rows,
   the same `playByPlay` reduction, the same Mexican League exclusion, the same shared
   `/feed/live` cache shortcut, four connections, outside the 06:00 to 11:00 UTC
   blackout. Sport 15 (A-) has had no season since 2020. The fetch dates gain 2026-10-15
   and 2026-10-16 under EW3-5; the blackout stays.
2. The census (`milb_pbp_census.py`): the probe and fetch seasons extend through 2026. The
   probe reads its six sampled games for the 2026 cells only and merges them into the
   existing probe block (the 2006 to 2025 cells are not re-read); the cache part is
   rebuilt over every cached game, as before.
3. The aggregate (`efv_w2_pbp.py`): `AGG_SEASONS` extends through 2026. A pool is used only
   when the census lists it as planned and complete (cached games at least the scheduled
   final games), and a game only when it is FULL (at least 3.0 pitches per PA): exactly
   the 2021 to 2024 construction. The counts, call codes, population z, k_resid slope,
   shrinkage K, NORM_MIN and NORM_MIN_LINES are unchanged; 2025 pools are reduced from the
   cache already on disk.

## 3. Census-complete, and the fallback (decided now)

A (level, season) pool of 2026 is census-complete when its cached games equal or exceed
its scheduled final games. If every planned 2026 pool is complete, AGG_SEASONS ends at
2026 and the live read uses 2026 and 2025. If any planned 2026 pool is not complete after
the fetch (a game the API cannot serve), AGG_SEASONS ends at 2025 and the live read takes
pitch events from 2025 and 2024, the last aggregated seasons; `_fam_pbp`'s receipt then
carries one line naming the incomplete pools and the seasons read. A partial 2026 pool is
never used.

## 4. What does not change

- No fit, slope, head, weight, lambda, constant or gate moves. The served heads were fit
  on training rows whose reads end at season 2023; extending the aggregate changes no
  training value.
- No development or reserved read changes: lv_dev and vm_dev read seasons 2022 and
  earlier; vm rows of 2025 or later decision years stay skipped unless the gate sha opens
  them; reserved list vintages still raise.
- Nothing served is regenerated by this task. `data/xfv.json` lights the family at its
  next rebuild by its own exporter; this task reports the live coverage it will read
  (rows with every column, per side) from a dry live read that writes nothing.

## 5. What is reported

Per 2025 and 2026 pool: scheduled final games, cached games, full games, qualifying
lines. Per side: live rows with every pitch-event column before (0) and after. The rater
panel family and the `scr_rpt_*` report keys are checked and what lighting them needs is
reported; neither is changed here.
