Trade Machine build + value a deal both ways: prospects on eFV surplus, MLB players on projection surplus · then the full Bayesian trade-value board · module by Anirudh Iyer
The rental leg has no floor. It prices the fraction of the season still to be played times the full-season surplus: the dollar value of a full season of projected WAR, minus the full-season salary. A negative number means projected production is worth less than the salary still owed, so the contract is underwater for the remainder, which is why salary-retention trades exist.
