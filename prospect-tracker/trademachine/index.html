Trade Machine β beta

One currency of surplus: prospects on eFV, MLB players on projections, and a debuted player no projection prices on his locked eFV backgrade (registry row xfv_backgrade, provisional)

Deal builder

How this works
  • One currency. Each player carries the surplus the rest of the site prints: a prospect's eFV surplus NPV, an MLB player's club control window surplus (guaranteed seasons in full, unsigned seasons through the fifth projected season).
  • Rental leg: remaining season fraction × full-season surplus (projected WAR value minus salary). No floor: negative means the salary still owed exceeds the production.
  • Verdict. Gap = |Side A − Side B| over the larger side. Under $5M reads balanced; otherwise it names the side that receives less. It predicts no club's answer.
  • Board. Bayesian surplus, contract-aware, positional scarcity, opt-out adjusted. A row's tooltip names its range and the basis of the range.
