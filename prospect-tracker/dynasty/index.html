Dynasty ◆ Diamond preview

Dynasty and keeper value on one board. Prospect grades come from eFV, the same model the PROSPX board runs on, blended with ETA and surplus value so a 19-year-old in A-ball and a 27-year-old in his arbitration years can sit in the same ranking. Call-up Watch flags who is next. The keeper calculator prices years of control against what the same player costs in a redraft league.
