    eephus.io, founder and lead developer

    • 2,340 commits, merges excluded, from 2026-05-17 to 2026-10-09.
    • 1,259 source files carry my @ARKNDJL header.

    Modules

    • PROSPX, 2026-05-17. The prospect board: 9,795 players tracked, 4,691 active, each graded on a consensus of seven ranking sources. Player cards, farm grades, draft and international boards. Spotlights co-created with Anirudh Iyer.
    • MLB Projections, 2026-06-10. A Marcel-style engine with LightGBM quantile heads. Gate 1: WAR MAE 0.516 against 0.624 for the 5/4/3 carry-forward, 20,721 player-seasons.
    • Free Agency, 2026-06-10. Years, guarantee and AAV on a deferral-adjusted NPV basis. Gate 2: guarantee MAE $9.85M against $10.74M for comps, 1,199 contracts over 9 winters.
    • CollegeSavant, 2026-06-11, co-created with Joseph Gillis. I carried his framework onto the site's theme and added the college to Single-A K% and BB% translation.
    • MLB Stats, 2026-06-30. Leaderboards for the plus stats below, with Leverage Index and win probability from play-by-play.
    • Alchemy Lab, 2026-06-30. Starter and reliever conversion fit scores and a pitch-add recommender. Registry: provisional.
    • Trade Machine, 2026-07-05. Dollar surplus per player and a trade evaluator, built on Anirudh Iyer's trade value engine.
    • Fantasy, 2026-07-19. Dynasty, redraft and DFS boards, with a combined dynasty rank in each of four Ottoneu formats.
    • Sports Betting and the Diamond, 2026-08-05. Model prices against the market and a shadow ledger of every posted selection.
    • Arbitration, 2026-08-09. Arb-1 salaries at MdAPE 0.184 against 0.339 for a one-WAR bar, 421 cases. The MLB Trade Rumors projection is closer: MdAPE 0.077 against 0.178 for this model on 808 identical settled cases. Recorded as a null.
    • Hall of Fame, 2026-09-06. Repaired the induction ledger, which was missing 130 of 354 inductees. Baseball-Reference's HoF Monitor beats every in-house column. Recorded as a loss.
    • In-Game Management, 2026-09-06. A descriptive ledger of nine decision families. No manager plus metric: club decision value failed its split-half gate.
    • Club Ledger, 2026-09-23. 30 club files, each row layer tested against the club roll-up.
    • Franchises, 2026-09-23. The Franchise Index puts eWAR, projected wins and farm grade on a descriptive 100 + 10z scale across the 30 clubs.
    • Also the Library (2026-06-10), MLB News (2026-06-20), Park Factors and History (2026-07-29) and Model Accountability (2026-08-07).

    Models and metrics

    • eFV, first shipped as xFV on 2026-06-22. Prospect Future Value fit to realized WAR from translated minor-league production, age for level and scout grades. Ties PROSPX consensus: Spearman difference -0.0049, CI95 -0.021 to +0.011 on the vintage-matched, timing-pinned frame (n 829, 2026-10-02).
    • xFV's rank win over consensus was retired on 2026-09-04, when the gap fell inside what the eval can resolve.
    • eWAR v2, 2026-08-04, with Will Cahoon. The Baseball-Reference run frame with pitching moved onto FIP: 22,196 player-seasons, 2011 to 2026. The label epoch of 2026-09-04 measured no accuracy gain. Recorded as a null.
    • fWAR replica, 2026-09-11. FanGraphs WAR rebuilt component by component from its public inputs. Registry: provisional.
    • The plus stats are empirical-Bayes shrunk indexes, each tested on the next season.
    • REM+, 2026-06-30: next-year RA9 rho 0.236 against 0.211 for raw FIP, 878 pairs.
    • SEM+, 2026-07-03: next-year RA9 rho 0.417 against 0.382 for raw FIP, 592 pairs.
    • HEM+, 2026-07-05: next-year wOBA rho 0.412 against 0.409 for raw wOBA, 3,372 pairs.
    • DEM+, 2026-09-11, pre-registered: next-year fielding rate rho 0.460 against 0.453 for the raw rate, 1,255 pairs.
    • KRP+ and RPS+ ship as display indexes, registered unvalidated. Swing+ is Will Cahoon's model; I wrote the ingest that scores tracked MLB swings with it.
    • Betting model, 2026-08-05. A run-scoring regression with a Skellam margin: on 777 vintage-dated games from 2026-08-01 to 2026-09-27, Brier 0.2427 against 0.2481 for the home-field constant (paired t 3.37). Registry: provisional.
    • Win expectancy, 2026-09-06. A team-blind state model on play-by-play. Registry: provisional.
    • Surplus value. Expected-value surplus with an isotonic map fit on resolved cohorts. Registry: validated.
    • Postseason bracket simulator, 2026-10-01. Exact series recursion over the posted bracket. Registry: unvalidated until its read after the World Series.
    • Postseason results ingest for F-SCORE, 2026-10-02. Every bracket from 2000, each checked against its identities.

    Data systems

    • Ingest for every FV source on the board: FanGraphs, MLB Pipeline, Baseball America, ESPN, The Athletic, Just Baseball and TJStats.
    • Game, Statcast, WAR and contract data from the MLB Stats API, Baseball Savant, Baseball-Reference and Spotrac.
    • A SQLite snapshot store of every ranking as published. Point-in-time vintages: a backtest reads only what was public on its date.
    • Identity resolution across MLBAM, FanGraphs and Baseball-Reference ids, with a repair ledger and collision tests.
    • Nightly and monthly pipelines that rebuild every artifact and page.

    Evaluation

    • Model registry: 351 rows on 2026-10-09. 54 validated, 159 provisional, 50 unvalidated, 88 deprecated. Each row names its eval artifact and a freshness clock. Losses stay on the record.
    • 125 gate documents, each written before its number is read.
    • Reserved frames: the 2020 and 2021 list vintages and the 2025 and later debut cohorts stay unscored until their registered read.
    • Claims tests: an accuracy claim on the site must come from an artifact, or the suite fails.
    • Source Comparison ledger in the Library: eFV against consensus and each outlet on the vintage-matched frame, 227 rows logged.
    • 807 test files, 11,528 test functions.

    Design

    • Pixel icon system, 2026-10-01. 79 sprite symbols on a pixel grid, generated from one source file.
    • Brand generator, 2026-10-02. The wordmark, favicons, app icons and share cards from one script.
    • Baseball-card and newspaper layouts in light and dark themes on one set of color tokens.

    Tools

    • Python (pandas, numpy, scipy, scikit-learn, LightGBM, statsmodels), SQLite, JavaScript, HTML and CSS, Playwright, git.

    Record

    • eWAR: instrumental in its creation. Designed the pitcher eWAR (pitcher-ewar-1.1.0), a run-value decomposition into fielding-independent runs, contact suppression, home-run opportunity and conversion, park and league adjustment and replacement level, over 4,371 MLB pitcher-seasons from 2021 to 2025.
    • eWAR: his milb-projections package (2026-07-18) holds the pitcher eWAR's MLB and MiLB export contracts and adapters, and his watermarked defense-plus code is the fielding leg (career defensive runs and WAR, catcher components). The merged eWAR the site has declared is his pitcher leg plus SDR fielding.
    • SDR: created it. A defensive run metric with range, arm, framing, blocking and double-play pivot components. Its method is his defense-plus pipeline, from reliability and latent range talent through position migration, MiLB translation and catcher components. eWAR's fielding leg waits on its data.
    • Swing+: created it. Bat speed, swing length, attack angle, path tilt and intercept point, scored on tracked MLB swings, 100 = league average. The site runs his model on player cards, the GFX rail and MLB Projections. The same tree holds Decision+, B2B+ and Hitter+.
    • Prospect defensive value: his thesis that a prospect's defensive home is a probability over positions, moved by body frame and age. The site's reconstruction carries his mark: Spearman 0.68 against 0.28 for the FanGraphs field grade at predicting realized MLB positional value, n 263.
    • MiLB Decision+: pitch extraction, zone calibration and level translation for minor-league swing decisions.
    • Code: 215 files carry his @willcolqhn header (swing-plus 104, playing-time-model 50, defense-plus 32, milb-decision-plus 17, advancement_pace 10, prospect-tracker 2). 25 commits, merges excluded, from 2026-05-16 to 2026-07-18.
    • Assisted with development of PROSPX: the first mock draft tool (2026-05-16, 7,934 lines), whose interface the PROSPX draft Simulate mode folds in; Advancement Pace, rebuilt as the AP+ panel on player cards; the position-migration thesis behind the position chips.
    • Assisted with development of MLB Projections and Free Agency: Swing+ on the projection cards, and a playing-time model (2026-06-13: call-up hazard, injury severity, depth-chart allocation) behind a playing-time risk layer that was tested in July 2026 and stays gated off.
    • Assisted with development of MLB Stats: clarified the SEM+ calibration sample in sem_plus.py (2026-07-15).
    • Assisted with development of CollegeSavant: the registry records his Stuff+ formula behind the college Stuff+ display.
    • Wrote Shortstops Galore: Why MLB Teams Love SS (2026-06-05).
