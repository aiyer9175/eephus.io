# Defensive positional adjustments

Fitted positional adjustments from eephus.io, published for Will's eWAR and SDR
work. Generated by:

    python prospect-tracker/tools/export_positional_ladder.py

**v1 is the current ladder.** It is the one Position Fit (the Alchemy Lab),
DEM+'s Pos (ours) and prospect defensive value use. v2 is a lab artifact under
re-read; do not take it up.

Files:

| file | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| `positional_ladder_v1.json` | v1, current: the ladder fitted on Baseball-Reference's positional runs, both bases, CIs, era splits, window sensitivity, the identification block, the folklore comparison |
| `positional_ladder_v1.csv` | v1's two ladders plus CI, one row per position |
| `positional_ladder_v2.json` | v2, lab artifact under re-read: the position-switcher ladder, both bases, CIs, its read, the verification that withdrew it, era and source splits |
| `positional_ladder_v2.csv` | v2's two bases plus CI, beside v1 and the FanGraphs table, one row per position |

Both are in runs per 650 PA of exposure, relative to the league-average
defensive position (an exposure-weighted zero over all nine rungs).

**Use the exposure-weighted column** unless you have a specific reason not to.
It is the basis that matches the Baseball-Reference frame eWAR rides, and it is
what DEM+'s Pos (ours) runs on.

The two columns differ by a single constant (1.48 runs), so every GAP between
two positions is identical in both. Only the zero point moves.

### Three sums, and only one of them is zero

This is the part that generates bug reports, so it is worth reading once.

- **Exposure-weighted over all nine rungs = 0.** Imposed by the fit. This is the
  only sum that is a constraint.
- **Equal-weight over nine rungs = -1.87.** Not a defect. DH is about 9% of
  league exposure, not one ninth, so equal-weighting over-counts its large
  negative rung.
- **Equal-weight over the eight fielders, on the exposure-weighted column =
  +11.83.** Also not a defect: once the weighted sum is pinned at zero, this
  value is forced arithmetically.

On the fielder-centred column the eight fielders do sum to zero, with DH outside
the sum. That is the folklore convention and it is why that column exists.

### What v1 is, and what it is not

The ladder is a least-squares **inversion** of Baseball-Reference's own
`runs_position` per 650 PA on per-row innings shares, no intercept, recentred so
the exposure-weighted sum is zero. Fit: 2021-2026, n=2,023
player-seasons, min 200 PA, R-squared 0.974.

So a rung reads as **what Baseball-Reference prices that position at**, not as
an independent measure of how hard the position is to play. v2 tried to be that
measure and is under re-read (below).

The rolling six-season window is a measured choice, not an assumption: it beat
the 2012-onward pool by 0.10 runs per 650 PA of held-out error (bootstrap
interval 0.079 to 0.115, better in 3 of 3 held-out seasons) under a
pre-registered forward-chained gate.

Era drift is real and larger than the sampling intervals, which is why the
window rolls. `era_splits` in the JSON carries the per-era rungs.

### The folklore comparison, and its trap

`folklore_comparison` in the v1 JSON holds the FanGraphs/Tango table everyone
quotes (C +12.5, SS +7.5, 2B/3B/CF +2.5, LF/RF -7.5, 1B -12.5, DH -17.5).

**Do not subtract it from ours directly.** That table is quoted per full
defensive season (162 games, 1,458 innings); ours is per 650 PA of exposure,
about 1,278 innings. The JSON carries both the raw table and a rescaled one,
and neither is presented as the answer: the convention is ambiguous across
sources, and the folklore table is the FanGraphs ladder while v1 inverts
Baseball-Reference.

## v2: the position-switcher ladder (lab artifact, under re-read)

Status: lab artifact, not served: refuted pending re-read (data/positional_ladder_v2_verify.json); Position Fit values with v1.

| Pos | Exposure-weighted | Fielder-centred | 95% CI |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| C | +10.19 | +9.53 | +10.00 to +10.40 |
| SS | +0.33 | -0.33 | -1.04 to +1.60 |
| 2B | -2.12 | -2.78 | -3.04 to -1.17 |
| 3B | -2.11 | -2.77 | -3.34 to -0.88 |
| CF | +1.42 | +0.76 | +0.05 to +2.63 |
| LF | +0.62 | -0.04 | -0.31 to +1.58 |
| RF | +0.32 | -0.34 | -0.74 to +1.40 |
| 1B | -3.37 | -4.03 | -4.53 to -2.23 |
| DH | -6.48 | -7.14 | -8.69 to -4.58 |

**What it tried to measure.** How many fielding runs the same players gain or
lose when they change position, on three fielding sources fit one at a time
(Baseball-Reference runs_field, FanGraphs fld, Statcast fielding runs
prevented) and pooled by inverse variance, seasons 2016-2026. For a player with 300 or more
fielded innings in two straight seasons, his fielding runs per 650 PA of
innings are fit on last season's rate, his age and the change in where he
played; Statcast adds same-season pairs.

**Its read.** One registered read (`prospect-tracker/docs/ewar/LADDER_V2_GATE.md`):
each season 2016-2026 held out in turn, predicting the fielding plus positional
runs of players who changed position, v2's error was 0.886 runs per 650 PA lower
than v1's refit on the same seasons (95% CI 0.717 to 1.059; 4,141 rows, 611 players). Next-season rows
alone: 0.754 (0.587 to 0.928). By source: Baseball-Reference 0.433, FanGraphs 0.709, Statcast 1.343.

**Why it was withdrawn.** The read's pass rule compared v2 only with v1. The
verification (`prospect-tracker/tools/ladder_v2_verify.py`) reran the same test
with a ladder that puts every fielding position at zero:

- Zero ties v2: zero's error was 0.049 runs per 650 PA higher than v2's
  (95% CI -0.005 to +0.103), and 0.837 lower than v1's. The read
  measured what v1's spread costs on this test, not a ladder v2 measured.
- Shrinking v1 toward zero lowers this test's error at every step (x1.0, x0.5, x0: 7.410, 6.742, 6.559);
  v2's error barely moves (6.524, 6.523, 6.559).
- The DH rung enters no scored row, and catcher barely enters one.
- Statcast's fielding runs are not centred within a position: per 1,277.7 innings
  the league books +4.30 in CF, -3.14 in LF and -1.63 in RF.
- The infield and outfield levels rest on the few dozen players who moved
  between them, and CF reads above SS on most source legs (CF minus SS: Baseball-Reference +7.50, FanGraphs +2.55, Statcast -0.62, Statcast centred +2.77).

**Catcher and DH.** Too few catchers change position to measure their rung (13 rows), so C keeps
v1's offset to the average fielder. DH is first base plus the first-base fielding of the
players who DH (-3.11 runs per 650 PA).

**Bases.** The two v2 bases differ by
one constant (+0.66 runs); only the exposure-weighted sum is zero
(equal-weight over nine rungs -1.20, over the eight fielders +5.28).

## Reliability constants

`signal_k_inn` in the v1 JSON carries the per-position innings constants
`ewar.py` already consumes for shrinkage. SDR needs an equivalent.

## Provenance

v1 is sliced read-only from `prospect-tracker/data/positional_fit.json`
(`fit.ladder`); v2 from `prospect-tracker/data/positional_ladder_v2.json`, the
record of its one read, `prospect-tracker/data/positional_ladder_v2_eval.json`,
and its verification, `prospect-tracker/data/positional_ladder_v2_verify.json`.
The exporter refits nothing. `source_generated_at` and `read_at` in the JSON
are the artifacts' own stamps.
